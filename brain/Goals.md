# Goals

What matters this stretch:

1. **Simple 2-Step Customer Flow:** Seamlessly add customers (Step 1: Enter name -> Step 2: Select services from the menu with dynamic total calculation).
2. **Flexible & Editable Price List:** Easily edit service prices in place and swipe to delete services with instant confirmation modals.
3. **Pastel Studio Visuals:** Maintain a cohesive, rounded aesthetic (cards, buttons, soft pink tones, bottom navigation with prominent center `+` button).
4. **Offline Local Autonomy:** Zero external backend blockers; all updates immediately reflect across IndexedDB and reactive hooks.
